# Aakaro demo script

Use the sample idea: “A platform that helps college students find compatible teammates for hackathons based on skills, interests and project goals.”

1. **Idea** — “I’m starting with a rough idea, and Aakaro gives it shape through a structured guided workflow.”
2. **Clarification** — “These three questions make the audience, difference, and personality explicit before AI proposes a brand.”
3. **Strategy** — “I review and lock the strategy, so the human chooses the foundation and AI assists from that confirmed brief.”
4. **Naming** — “Aakaro generates and evaluates five real candidates, then lets me shortlist exactly two.”
5. **Graveyard** — “The three rejected candidates stay visible in The Graveyard with their actual trade-offs instead of disappearing.”
6. **Directions** — “I compare two distinct expressions tied to the two shortlisted names and lock the direction I want.”
7. **Brand Kit** — “The locked direction becomes an editable kit with colors, typography, voice, and explicit Brand Rules.”
8. **Spellcheck** — “I paste intentionally off-brand copy and each issue points back to the exact confirmed rule that it violates.”
9. **Apply Fix** — “I apply only the fixes I accept; the working content changes, while the original pasted content is never silently overwritten.”
10. **Export** — “After re-checking to a clean result, I can download Markdown or use the browser Print/Save as PDF path.”

Sample clarification answers:

- “First-time participants who want a welcoming, skill-balanced team.”
- “Match people by skills and project goals before they form a team.”
- “Collaborative and welcoming, with enough energy for a live hackathon.”

Sample off-brand copy:

> Our revolutionary, game-changing platform is the best-in-class solution for every student.

Keep the demo in the visible mock/dev mode unless real Gemini and Firebase credentials have been configured and verified.
